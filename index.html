Free English-language TV. Over a thousand channels, each one checked to be working when the list was last updated. You watch them with VLC media player.

Watch on a computer, phone or tablet

1. Get VLC media player

VLC is free and plays the channels. It works on Windows, Mac and Linux.

Download VLC

On a phone or tablet, install VLC from the App Store or Google Play.

2. Open the channels

Windows: download the Revell T.V shortcut and double-click it. Save it on the desktop and it works like an app.

Revell T.V for Windows

If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. If VLC is missing, the shortcut opens the VLC website for you.

Mac, Linux, phone or tablet: open VLC and choose Open Network Stream (Windows and Linux: the Media menu; Mac: the File menu; phone: the Network tab). Paste this address and press Play.

On Linux you can also save revell-tv.desktop into ~/.local/share/applications to get a Revell T.V icon in the menu.

3. Pick a channel

  • The first channel starts playing. Press Ctrl+L to see the whole list (Mac: Window menu, Playlist).
  • Double-click a channel to watch it. To find one, type in the search box, for example “news”.
  • Double-click the picture for full screen, and again to leave it.

Free channels go offline now and then, or only play in some countries. If one does not start, try another.

The list always opens in its newest version, so there is nothing to update. Channels that failed the last check wait in the penalty box until they work again.

Channels come from the public iptv-org list, narrowed down to English. Revell T.V does not host any of them.
